As a teaching assistant for CS 311, I mentored more than **400 students** in the
mathematical foundations of computer science.

Topics included propositional logic, sets, combinatorics, graph theory and
algorithm verification. I particularly enjoyed helping students turn an intuitive
idea into a clear, structured argument.
