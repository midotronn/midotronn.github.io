I am a master's student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin,
graduating in May 2027. I received my bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and
Mathematics from UT Austin in May 2025.

My research focuses on efficient, physically grounded embodied AI, including
reconstructing and generating physically plausible human and robot motion, and on
giving people meaningful control over AI systems. I have worked
as a research intern at Georgia Tech's EIC Lab, supervised by
[Prof. Yingyan (Celine) Lin](https://eiclab.scs.gatech.edu/pages/team.html) and
[Prof. Chaojian Li](https://sponge-lab.github.io/), and at UT Austin's VITA Lab,
supervised by [Prof. Atlas Wang](https://ece.utexas.edu/people/faculty/atlas-wang)
and [Dr. Hezhen (Alex) Hu](https://alexhu.top/).

I am interested in PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027. I am always happy to
discuss research and potential collaborations.
