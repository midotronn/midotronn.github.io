Teaching Assistant, The University of Texas at Austin

I mentored more than 400 students in the mathematical foundations of computer science, including propositional logic, sets, combinatorics, graph theory and algorithm verification.

I enjoyed helping students connect an intuitive idea to a clear mathematical argument, and understand how that reasoning applies to programs and algorithms.
