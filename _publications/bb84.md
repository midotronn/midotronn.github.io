**Mohammed Hassan and Omar Abouelazm**

This work proposes storing received qubits in quantum memory and delaying
measurement until authenticated basis information is available. In the idealized
analysis, this avoids the basis-mismatch discards associated with random
measurement-basis selection.

The paper discusses quantum-memory coherence, timing, authenticated communication
and practical limitations. Its efficiency argument concerns an idealized protocol,
not a demonstrated doubling of end-to-end secret-key throughput in a deployed
quantum network.

The research was carried out from August to October 2024. The work was
**accepted as a poster at Quantum Information Processing (QIP) 2025 in
Raleigh, North Carolina**, listed as "Improving BB84 via Quantum Memory" in the
[official accepted-poster program](https://qip2025.duke.edu/accepted-posters/).
The manuscript is publicly available on arXiv.
