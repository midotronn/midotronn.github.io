I am a master's student in Computer Science at
[The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/), graduating in
May 2027. I received my bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics
from UT Austin in May 2025.

My research focuses on efficient multimodal and embodied AI, and on interactive
systems that give people meaningful control over generated outputs. I have worked
as a research intern at [Georgia Tech's EIC Lab](https://eiclab.scs.gatech.edu/),
supervised by Prof. Yingyan (Celine) Lin and Prof. Chaojian Li on T3R, and at
[UT Austin's VITA Lab](https://vita-group.github.io/), supervised by
Prof. Atlas Wang and Dr. Hezhen (Alex) Hu on MAESTRO.

I am the first author of [T3R]({{ '/publication/t3r/' | relative_url }}),
a training-free framework for efficient and robust vision-language-action
models, accepted at ASP-DAC 2027. My current work also includes
[MAESTRO]({{ '/publication/maestro/' | relative_url }}), an agentic system for
long-form music-to-3D dance composition and editing, under review at CHI 2027.

I am interested in PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to
[email me](mailto:{{ site.data.profile.email }}) about research or collaborations.
You can find more detail in my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})
and [CV]({{ site.data.profile.cv | relative_url }}).
