**Lab research project**

MIRA-Quant explores how a multi-agent iterative-reasoning system can support
automated large-language-model quantization.

I developed the system as a co-author during my research internship at
**Georgia Tech's EIC Lab**, supervised by Prof. Yingyan (Celine) Lin.
The evaluated system matches expert-tuned NVIDIA Model Optimizer references
within **0.3 perplexity** across **15 configurations**, spanning five LLMs and
three post-training quantization methods.

The project complements my work on VLA token refinement by approaching model
efficiency through automated quantization.
